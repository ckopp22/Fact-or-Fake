FACT or FAKE

True... or totally made up?

How to play

  1. Pick who's playing and the score you're playing to.
  2. A card shows a statement. Read it out loud.
  3. Everyone locks in an answer: Fact or Fake. Thumbs up or down, a hand signal, or write it down.
  4. Tap the card to reveal the truth.
  5. Everyone who was right taps their own name for a point. Tap fast!
  6. Tap anywhere else for the next card. First to the target score wins.
